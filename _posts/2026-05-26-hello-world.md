Public log of what I'm learning in security. Writeups, notes, and
things that didn't behave the way I expected.

## Why write

The half-life of a half-understood technique is about six weeks. I keep
relearning the same stack canaries and format-string offsets, so
writing them down forces me past the hand-wave into something I can
actually re-derive. Side effect: future me gets a grep-able corpus
instead of a tab pile in Notes.app. Anyone else reading is a bonus.

## What lives here

- `ctf/` — per-box writeups (HTB, THM, picoCTF). Retired boxes only.
- `notes/` — concept dumps once I can re-derive them.
- `tools/` — scripts I wrote and why.
- `reading/` — paper and book summaries.
- `journal/` — monthly progress, rabbit holes included.

## House rules

- No active vuln details without coordinated disclosure.
- No client, employer, or coursework material that isn't explicitly
  mine to share.
- One writeup per week beats five then silence.
